## 1 · Pace-chart trend line (Analysis → Pace)

- **What:** a dashed straight line over the pace chart, through the window's finished days (today
  left out — half-written, it would drag the line down), and a fourth figure under the chart beside
  total · avg/day · peak day: **320 → 540** *trend* — the line's value on the window's first day and on
  yesterday, in words a day. Hidden when the finished days hold no words; both ends shown at 0 rather
  than below.
- **Standard:** a linear least-squares trend line, Excel's and Google Sheets' default trendline;
  computed with **simple-statistics** `linearRegression` (ISC, simple-statistics.github.io) —
  `^7.12.1`, not 8.0.0, which was published 2026-10-28.
- **Kit row:** none (no statistics job in §19); the repo's other trend (`voiceDrift.js` `trend`, early
  third vs late third with a threshold) classifies drift, it doesn't draw a line — a different job.
- **Blast radius:** `paceSeries` — `src/pages/AnalysisPage.vue:53`, `src/services/analysis.js:126`;
  nothing else reads it.
- **Test:** `src/services/analysis.test.js` — the trend leaves out the last day, clamps at 0, and is
  null when no finished day has words.
- **Docs:** `docs/analysis.md` (Pace), `docs/whats-new.md`, the line leaves `docs/roadmap.md`.

## 2 · Multi-window

- **What (JustWrite's side):** a command-palette action **Open this page in a new window**, and
  **Ctrl/⌘+Shift+N** (VS Code's New Window): the current page opens in a second window of the desktop
  app — a new tab in a browser; not offered on the phone.
- **Standard:** Electron's `window.open` → `setWindowOpenHandler` with `overrideBrowserWindowOptions`
  (electronjs.org/docs/latest/api/window-open) — the kit's shell, HELD 1 in the kit's tracker,
  released to the kit's session (just-core-09) 2026-10-29. The app's call, as the kit will name it in
  §19: `window.open(router.resolve(to).href, "_blank", "noopener")` — a window of the app on the
  desktop, a tab in a browser; hidden on the phone. The kit's own open questions (closing the main
  window, a new window's size and place, the `null` origin fix) are on its list for the user. Another window's changes: §19 "Another window changed something"
  (`ChangeCounter`, `watchOtherWindows`), already in `src/boot/jw.js:239-260` — a window reloads the
  open book once its cursor leaves a text box (ruled 2026-10-15), and the server merges both windows'
  saves.
- **Attack:** the sidebar's entries are `router.push` on click, not links (`Sidebar.vue`: 0 `href`), so
  Shift+click can't open a window for free; making them links is a separate change, not proposed.
- **Blast radius:** `src/components/CommandPalette.vue` (the `act:*` list), `src/layouts/MainLayout.vue:164`
  (the one keydown handler); `docs/keyboard-shortcuts.md` has no Ctrl+Shift+N.
- **Test:** the palette action calls `window.open` with the current hash route (unit); on the built
  app, a second window opens on the same page (once, with the kit's commit).
- **Docs:** `docs/keyboard-shortcuts.md`, `docs/writing.md`, `docs/whats-new.md`, the line leaves
  `docs/roadmap.md`.

## 5 · "Repeated words" highlight

- **What:** Writing settings gets **Highlight repeated words** beside the other three, with **within
  [50] words**. A word marked when it appears again within that many words in the same paragraph —
  every occurrence in the cluster, a green underline, hover "Used again within 50 words". Words on the
  standard English stop-word list (the, and, was, he… 108 words) and the story bible's names are left
  out; the same form only (*walk* and *walked* are different words). English books only, like the other
  three, so the English-only note reads "These four lists…".
- **NOT STANDARD — the scan.** No maintained package finds echoes within a window: retext-repeated-words
  and write-good catch only a doubled word ("the the"). The scan is ours (a few lines in
  `styleMetrics.js` beside `filterWordRanges`), drawn by the existing `editorDecorations.js`, which
  works one paragraph at a time — why the window stops at the paragraph. The skip list IS standard:
  **stopword** 3.1.5 (MIT, github.com/fergiemcdowall/stopword), its `eng`.
- **Blast radius:** `src/components/EditorSettingsModal.vue:44,113,118`,
  `src/components/RichEditor.vue:25,123,140,2031-2037`, `src/services/proseHighlights.js:18,30`,
  `src/services/editorSettings.js` (`DEFAULT_EDITOR_SETTINGS`).
- **Test:** `src/services/analysis/styleMetrics.test.js` — a repeat inside the window is marked,
  outside it isn't, a stop word and a story name never are.
- **Docs:** `docs/writing.md` (Prose highlights), `docs/whats-new.md`.
