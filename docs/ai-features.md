# The AI features

Everything the AI does in JustWrite, in one place. Every feature below is a row
under **Settings → AI → Routing by feature** with its own engine preset — one
model can serve all of them, or a heavy feature can point at a stronger model
while quick ones stay local. How routing, presets and providers work is
**[AI providers](ai-providers.md)**; this page is what the features *are*.

## Writing

- **Writer actions** — the AI menu in each scene's strip: Rewrite, Expand,
  Tighten, Continue, Describe, plus all Line edits.

## Drafting tools

- **Research feel** — a structured sensory pack (smell / sound / touch / …)
  for a selected subject — the editor's "Research feel" modal.
- **Unstuck** — five ways to unblock the current scene: goal shift, interrupt,
  setting, reveal, time cut.
- **Brainstorm** — the Brainstorm view: name / title / freeform idea
  generation with thumbs-up steering.

## Analysis

- **Critique** — the Critique modal: line-level Notes (flags / suggestions /
  observations) and the Structure pass (tension, hook, pacing, ending).
- **Multi-reader panel** — four reader personas (genre reader, literary
  critic, agent intern, book-club reader) react to a chapter in parallel.

## Whole book

- **Plot-hole audit** — whole-book continuity scan for contradictions,
  timeline issues, and character-knowledge errors.
- **Reverse outline** — reads the whole draft and produces the act structure
  the book actually has: plot points, act breaks, per-chapter beats.
- **Beat sheet** — maps your draft to Save the Cat, Hero's Journey, or 7-Point
  Story Structure beats.
- **Marketing pack** — logline, back-cover blurbs, synopsis, and elevator
  pitch for querying and pitching.
- **Foreshadowing scan** — whole-book scan for setups that may not have paid
  off.
- **Reader knowledge** — tracks dramatic irony: what the reader knows vs.
  what the POV character knows, chapter by chapter.
- **Voice drift** — diagnoses what shifted between an outlier chapter and
  your baseline voice, in the Analysis dashboard.

## Characters

- **Entity sweep** — scans chapters for new characters, locations and objects
  to add to the story bible.
- **Character audit** — per-character consistency audit (profile + their
  scenes → flagged actions) on the Characters view.
- **Character profile** — drafts a character's profile fields (description,
  motivation, arc, backstory) from their scenes — reviewed before anything
  saves.
- **Character voice** — drafts how a character speaks (register, rhythm,
  verbatim sample lines) from their actual dialogue — reviewed before
  anything saves.
- **Relationship arc** — chapter-by-chapter warmth / tension / power tracking
  for a pair of characters.

## Chat

- **Ask the book** — RAG question/answer over your manuscript, in the chat
  panel.
- **Character chat** — the chat panel's "Talk to a character" mode:
  first-person, in-voice answers from your cast.

## Home

- **Resume briefing** — generates the Home "Previously on your novel" recap
  card.
- **Session recap** — the end-of-day "Wrap up session" recap with open-thread
  suggestions.

## Where each one runs

**Settings → AI → Routing by feature** shows every row above with the preset it
resolves to right now. Open a row to read and edit its prompt, test it in the
Lab, and point it at a different preset — the mechanics are covered in
[AI providers](ai-providers.md#routing-features-to-specific-providers).
