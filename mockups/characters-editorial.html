Principal · POV character

Aldis Vane

Main character Role Cartographer's apprentice Age 19 Vane household

She would rather redraw the world than admit her father lied about it.

Motivation
Wants

To finish and sell her father's last chart, and clear the debts that came with it.

Needs

To accept that some maps are made of grief, not coastline.

The lie she believes

If she is precise enough, the truth won't matter.

The truth she meets

Accuracy was never the same thing as honesty.

Arc
1Beginning

Dutiful and exact, certain her father's work is gospel — and hiding her own doubt.

2Midpoint

The false coast is exposed at sea. She keeps sailing toward it anyway.

3End

Burns the false chart and draws the first honest map of her life.

Voice & dialect
Accent
Clipped harbour-town
Vocabulary
Precise; nautical & cartographic
Speech tic
Counts under her breath when frightened.
"Three days of water and a coast that doesn't exist. Plot it anyway."
Backstory

Raised in the loft above her father's drafting room, Aldis learned to read soundings before she could read words. When Tomas Vane died mid-commission, the Guild called in his debts and his half-finished chart of the southern coast — the one nobody else could complete.

She took the work to keep the house. She did not yet know the coast on it had never been surveyed.
