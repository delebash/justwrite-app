Three ways the Home could go.

Three full-page mockups of alternative Home views — each picks one anti-slop bet and commits to it. All three reuse the app's existing design tokens, fonts, and light/dark theme, so nothing breaks if you bring elements back to HomeView.vue.

What I'm trying to avoid

The current Home is tasteful — but dashboard-shaped.

  • Goal-ring + sparkline + DoW bars + heatmap reads as a generic productivity dashboard before it reads as a writing tool.
  • Several stat tiles in a bottom row compete for attention without a clear primary action.
  • "Resume writing" lives in a gold card mid-page — strong, but ringed by widgets that pull eye-traffic.
  • Descriptive paragraph at the top explains what Home is — a sign the design is doing too much.
  1. ACaptain's Log→

    A page of prose where a dashboard used to be.

    Home is a journal entry written by the system: yesterday's word count and last-touched chapter, what you're picking up at, what's pending — set in Fraunces, one column, no widgets. Stats are behind a "stats" toggle for the days you want them.

    • Dashboard widgets
    • Goal rings & sparklines
    • Help-text explanations
    • Editorial single column
  2. BManuscript Spine→

    No Home view at all — open straight to the book.

    Open the app and land on the manuscript itself. The TOC spine occupies the left, the current chapter the centre, a calm inspector the right. Today's session, streak, and goal collapse into one hairline footer. The dashboard is replaced by the artefact it was about.

    • Separate landing surface
    • Goal rings
    • Atmospheric backgrounds
    • Spine · pane · inspector
  3. CPaper Shelf→

    A shelf of chapter cards, oldest left, working right.

    Home as a long horizontal shelf of chapter cards — each card a quick read of title, status, words, and a single line of opener prose. The chapter you last wrote in has a hairline gold edge and is centred on load. Stats live in the right margin as a newspaper-style colophon, not a dashboard.

    • Status-tile clutter
    • Bottom widget row
    • Generic stat cards
    • Horizontal artefact shelf
